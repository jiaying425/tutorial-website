Ye Alin (叶阿林)

Ye Alin is a distinguished contemporary Chinese painter specialising in guohua (Chinese ink painting). Born in 1958 in Yiwu, Zhejiang Province, he is currently Vice Director of the Creative Committee of the Beijing Mingshi Poetry, Calligraphy and Painting Institute.

A student of renowned masters Ya Ming (亚明) and Cheng Shifa (程十发), Ye Alin developed a signature style he calls the "New Zhejiang-style painting" (新浙派风情画) — blending the classic literati tradition of the Zhejiang school with a fresh, lyrical sensibility. His landscapes and figure paintings are celebrated for their atmospheric depth, delicate ink-wash technique, and evocative depictions of Jiangnan scenery.

In 2013, the first major domestic exhibition of his New Zhejiang-style paintings was held in Beijing, accompanied by the inauguration of the First Zhejiang Wind Zhejiang School Art Research Association. His monograph Collection of Chinese Modern Famous Painters — Ye Alin was published in 2018, cementing his standing among China's leading contemporary painters.

Career Milestones

1958

Born in Yiwu, Zhejiang

Ye Alin was born in Yiwu, Zhejiang Province — a region with a rich artistic heritage that would profoundly shape his lifelong passion for ink painting and the Jiangnan landscape.

Art Education

Mentored by Masters Ya Ming & Cheng Shifa

Ye Alin studied under two giants of 20th-century Chinese painting: Ya Ming (亚明), the celebrated Jiangsu guohua master, and Cheng Shifa (程十发), the renowned Shanghai school figure and flower-bird painter.

2013

First Major Exhibition in Beijing

Ye Alin's New Zhejiang-style paintings (新浙派风情画) were unveiled at their first major domestic exhibition in Beijing, coinciding with the founding of the First Zhejiang Wind Zhejiang School Art Research Association.

2014

Publication in People's Servant

His work Wang Zhaojun Leaving the Frontier (昭君出塞) was published in the prestigious journal People's Servant (《人民公仆》), Issue 3 of 2014, bringing his paintings to a wide national readership.

2015

Monograph Published by Tianjin Fine Arts

Tianjin Fine Arts Publishing House published his personal monograph Appreciation of Contemporary Chinese Painting Masters — Ye Alin's Chinese Painting Collection, establishing him among China's pre-eminent contemporary ink painters.

2018

Major Monograph Published

Publication of Collection of Chinese Modern Famous Painters — Ye Alin (《中国近现代名家画集-叶阿林》), a comprehensive survey of his career and works, affirming his legacy in the history of modern Chinese painting.

Present

Vice Director, Beijing Mingshi Institute

Ye Alin currently serves as Vice Director of the Creative Committee of the Beijing Mingshi Poetry, Calligraphy and Painting Institute, continuing to paint, teach, and champion the New Zhejiang school tradition.
