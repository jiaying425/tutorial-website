The Artist

Ye Alin · 叶阿林

Ye Alin (叶阿林)

Ye Alin is a celebrated contemporary Chinese artist renowned for her vivid oil paintings that fuse folk tradition with modern expressionism. Born in Yunnan Province, her work draws deeply from the rich tapestry of China's ethnic minority cultures, landscapes, and daily village life.

Her paintings are characterised by bold, luminous colour palettes and a compassionate eye for the human figure — particularly women and children of China's rural communities. She captures fleeting moments of grace with an immediacy that feels both timeless and deeply personal.

Ye Alin has exhibited widely across mainland China and internationally, earning recognition from the China Artists Association and institutions devoted to the preservation and promotion of folk-rooted fine art.

Origin Yunnan Province, China
Medium Oil on Canvas · Watercolour
Style Folk Realism · Expressionism
Member China Artists Association

"Every brushstroke carries the memory of a place, a face, a feeling that must not be forgotten."

— Ye Alin

The Miao Festival Series

Series · Oil on Canvas

Terraced Fields at Dawn

An expansive landscape of the Yuanyang rice terraces flooded with the first light of dawn, reflecting sky and cloud in still water.

Landscape · Oil on Canvas

Grandmother's Loom

An intimate portrait of a Dai elder at her traditional loom, celebrating the intergenerational transmission of craft and memory.

Portrait · Oil on Canvas

Journey

Career Milestones

Early Years

Born in Yunnan

Ye Alin grew up surrounded by the lush mountains and vibrant folk culture of Yunnan Province, which would shape her artistic vision for life.

Art Education

Formal Training

Studied fine arts at a leading Chinese art institution, where she honed her technique in oil painting and developed her distinctive expressive style.

First Exhibition

National Debut

Her debut solo exhibition in Beijing introduced her folk-infused realism to the national art scene, earning critical acclaim and public attention.

Association Membership

China Artists Association

Elected as a member of the China Artists Association — a recognition of her enduring contribution to Chinese contemporary fine art.

International Stage

Global Exhibitions

Works exhibited in France, the United States, and Southeast Asia, broadening international appreciation of folk-rooted Chinese painting.

Present

Continued Legacy

Ye Alin continues to paint, teach, and advocate for the preservation of ethnic minority cultures through her art — a living bridge between tradition and modernity.
